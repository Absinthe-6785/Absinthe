## Absinthe strengths

- Integrated Archive + Trace for study/history workflows (differentiator)
- Block editor with academic blocks (math, footnotes, mermaid) without plugin sprawl
- K-30.41–42 relationship + maintenance surfaces in-product
- No AI lock-in; local-first friendly

## Absinthe weaknesses

- No plugin ecosystem
- Review still manual — no SRS (intentionally deferred)
- Mobile maintenance surfaces limited
- Citation/bibliography tooling immature vs Obsidian Pandoc/Zotero workflows
